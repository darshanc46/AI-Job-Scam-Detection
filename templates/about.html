About JobShyld AI

Project Overview

JobShyld AI is an AI-powered Job & Internship Scam Detection Platform that helps students identify fake recruitment offers using NLP and Machine Learning classification.

Our Mission

To protect students and job seekers from online recruitment fraud by providing a simple, fast, and reliable AI-based verification system.

Our Vision

To create a safer digital recruitment environment where every student can confidently apply for genuine jobs and internships.

Key Features

✔ ML & NLP Scam Detection
✔ Multi-Factor Risk Analysis
✔ Recruiter Email Audit
✔ Dynamic Risk Visualizations

Technologies Used

Python 3.10+
Flask Framework
Scikit-Learn & NLTK
HTML5 / Vanilla CSS3 / JavaScript
