Essential guidelines to avoid falling victim to recruitment scams

🚨 5 Red Flags of Fake Job & Internship Offers

  • 1. Money Demands: Any request for registration fees, laptop security deposits, or training kit fees is 100% a scam.
  • 2. Chat-Only Hiring: Recruiters who insist on interviewing solely via Telegram or WhatsApp without formal video/voice calls.
  • 3. Generic Email Domains: Official corporate job offers coming from @gmail.com or @yahoo.com accounts.
  • 4. Unrealistic Pay: Offers promising $500/day or $100/hr for simple typing or copy-paste tasks with zero experience.
  • 5. Instant Selection: Job offers extended immediately without reviewing your resume or interviewing.
